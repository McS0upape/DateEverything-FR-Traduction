- **Le saviez-vous ?** : Impossibly positive in the most dreary of situations, Holly is a ray of sunshine in sunless spaces. She's messy, disorganised, and easily excitable about any-which-what flavor of the month is the current talking point. The kind of person who starts preparing for Blissmas the day after Hollyween. She's also wildly distractible, and while she adores her found family, she's terrible at scheduling time to be with them. Notorious for always working holidays, she's stuck in a cycle of both adoring festivities, but never allowing herself to enjoy them.
