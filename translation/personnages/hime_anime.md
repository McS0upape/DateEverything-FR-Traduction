- **Le saviez-vous ?** : "STARLIGHT SMASH!" Hero-Hime is the protagonist of Starlight Overheaders! Hero-hime is energetic, goofy, and overly positive in an endearing way, or at least, that's what the animators hoped. She carries her magic tennis racket, which invokes her transformation between sports hero and average college student. Her goal is to become the TENNISTAR - the best tennis player and hero of Heart Ace Academy. Hero-Hime trains diligently every day in order to achieve her goal. She's an underdog, after all, and she knows.
