- **N'aime pas** : Finding Solutions
- **Le saviez-vous ?** : Jerry is a slightly neurotic hoarder, er 'curator', who has trouble letting go of any item. He worries that he might need something further down the line, no matter how useless it is. The idea that he could be throwing away something with the slightest chance of being even tangential to being useful is mortifying to him, so he keeps everything. A drawer of habits.
