- **Le saviez-vous ?** : Our girl Chairemi here is a dedicated LARPer, a jill-of-all-trades useful in any situation! She prides herself on being able to fit into whatever role is needed to make things run smoothly: A stern office chair, a matronly rocking chair, a boozy lounge chair for late evenings, you name it! Chairemi is almost always in character as something or other. So much so that no one is really sure what her actual personality is like. One might think she's trying to hide something... but no! She's just a theater kid through and through. She puts a little bit of herself into every character she plays, and only someone who meets her on her level will notice. Chairemi holds actor comfort and safety above all else.
