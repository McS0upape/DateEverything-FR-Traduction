- **Le saviez-vous ?** : Bathsheba is welcoming, warm, and always there for you at the end of a long day. She is all for comfy wind-downs - scented candles, a DVD of Legally Blinds or Never Been Dished, and an offer to paint your nails or braid your hair. And while she can be pretty chill, she can also get right into it with you when you need someone to vent to. However, she also tends to feel somewhat isolated and disconnected where she is in the house and has a pretty bad case of FOMO over the others.
