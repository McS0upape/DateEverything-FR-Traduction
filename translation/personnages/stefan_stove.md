- **Le saviez-vous ?** : Stefan is a big burly stove who despite his hard exterior has a soft side hidden beneath those layers of muscle. If there's one thing that reigns supreme for Stefan it is that food is the glue of the world. It brings everyone together, from all walks of life, which instils a passion burning inside him. That passion makes him come off as fiery and overbearing sometimes. He is constantly bossing the kitchen around (not that anyone actually pays attention) so that everything runs like a well-oiled machine. However, behind that veritable wall of fire is a stove who simply wants to provide joy to all those around him.
