- **Le saviez-vous ?** : Tydus feels that the weight of the world rests on her because she alone must decide whether to sacrifice her darling detergent pods. She views the task of guarding over her pods as a higher purpose, and without them she feels literally empty. Though, she knows just as well as anybody that her pods are the only 4-in-1 laundry solution with highly concentrated detergent, stain remover, and color protector, outperforming the leading laundry detergent and giving you more bang for your buck. Tydus is loyal to a fault and will do absolutely anything for the ones she loves most. Careful though, she may be used to washing off crap, but she refuses to take any of yours.
