- **Le saviez-vous ?** : These two best friends have been together through thick and thin, but always draped in the best drip. The only thing they love more than fashion is the good gossip. Is this annoying? Sometimes. But they don't care. The neighborhood is their entertainment and they wouldn't have it any other way. Their relationship with each other comes above everything and to get in good with one, you have to get in good with the other. Curt is older by 7 minutes, and while they are not brothers, he takes on the role of one. Rod looks up to Curt and thinks that no one is good enough for him. If you can't make them laugh, they don't want to hear it. Come with a joke or don't come at all.

## Voix en français

- **Genre grammatical du personnage** : Curt et Rod sont deux hommes (il). Dialogue à deux : accords masculins.
- **Tutoie ou vouvoie le joueur ou la joueuse** : tutoie (comme tout le monde).
- **Le joueur ou la joueuse lui parle avec** : tu
- **Registre** : très familier, argot de rue années 90, tchatche de potes. Curt est le plus snob et sûr de lui, Rod le plus brut et taquin.
- **Personnalité, manière de parler** : duo d'amis inséparables qui se « charrient » en permanence (throwing shade = chambrer, tacler avec classe). Piques de mode et de tissu (coutures, drapé, ourlets). Surnoms au joueur ou à la joueuse : épicènes (« toi », « chef », « boss ») ou via {{...}}.
