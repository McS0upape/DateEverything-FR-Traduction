- **Le saviez-vous ?** : Maggie is always on the hunt for the next mystery. Whether it's trying to figure out where that table coaster disappeared to (you lost it), or who has been leaving the refrigerator door open at 2am (also you), or who forgot to turn the oven off before leaving on that trip to Aruba (again, you), Maggie is on the case! But there might be a dark side to Maggie's investigative spirit. Years ago, Maggie was in a relationship with a beautiful pair of binoculars. She fell madly, utterly in love. Unfortunately, the binoculars were unfaithful to her, and Maggie has been left trying to protect herself by finding out everything she can about any possible partner. This has left her pushing away anyone who might get close, as she has no sense of when "being interested" has become "being a snoop."
